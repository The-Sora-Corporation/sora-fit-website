Sora Fit Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026.

This policy covers the current Sora Fit iOS development version and this support website. Live advertising and release purchases are not enabled. We will update this policy before introducing live advertising or materially changing our data practices.

Who operates Sora Fit

Sora Fit is operated by Ben Fong (legal name: Benjamin Fong), an independent developer, not a registered company. For privacy questions or requests, contact sorafitsupport@gmail.com.

Your tracking information

Sora Fit stores the information you enter and your companion settings on your device. This includes:

  • Your self-declared age group (under 18, 18–19, or 20+); we do not collect an exact age or birthday.
  • Weight entries and dates, starting weight, height, and preferred measurement units, where available for your age group.
  • Optional body-fat readings and dates.
  • Habit check-ins, custom win names, which default wins you have hidden, and companion celebration history. Removed wins and their history are retained locally for restoration.
  • Cat color, sleep and wake times, and optional reminder settings.
  • Whether body mirroring is enabled and your confirmations that you are at least 20 years old and are not pregnant.

The app uses this information locally to show your history, calculate BMI-based artistic cat shapes, react to habits, and remember your preferences. It does not request your date of birth. Body mirroring is optional and is not a medical assessment.

We do not operate a server that receives these tracking records, and the app does not send them to RevenueCat or Google AdMob. There is no Sora Fit login, cloud-sync service, public profile, or social feed in this version. The app does not read Apple Health records or request location, contacts, camera, or microphone access for these features.

Your device's operating-system backups or device-transfer services may include app data depending on your settings. These services are separate from Sora Fit. We cannot retrieve lost local tracking records for you.

Adults-only access and age groups

Sora Fit is intended only for adults ages 18 and older. Before opening the main app, we ask you to confirm an age group and save that range only on your device. We do not send the selected range to RevenueCat or AdMob. This is self-declaration, not identity verification or parental consent.

  • Under 18: this version does not provide access to the app, including the companion, habits, measurements and purchase restoration. On a fresh session, the stop screen does not initialize ads or purchase services. Older prototype selections of under 13 or 13–17 are also blocked, without erasing existing records.
  • Ages 18–19: optional measurement tracking is available, but body mirroring remains unavailable.
  • Ages 20+: body mirroring is a separate opt-in with eligibility confirmations. Cat reactions reward habits, never weight loss.

You can update your range through Companion settings → Age & privacy, or Update age group on the under-18 stop screen, when you move into another group or need to correct a mistake. You must select and confirm your current range; cancelling or selecting under 18 again keeps access blocked. Because no birthday is stored, ranges do not advance automatically. Confirmed changes apply automatically once saved, without a reload. When changing an adult profile to under 18 in the native app, access is blocked and you must fully close the app to end any previously initialized purchase or ad service session; a JavaScript reload cannot unload those native services. An age correction cannot recall data already transmitted. Existing tracking records are preserved locally but hidden when the selected age group cannot access them. The app has one local profile, not separate accounts for people sharing a device.

If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about a child's use or information sent to us, contact the privacy email above. Please do not send unnecessary health records or identity documents.

Purchases and RevenueCat

For age groups 18 and older, Sora Fit integrates Apple's purchase system and RevenueCat to retrieve product information, check purchase access, validate transactions, and restore an eligible purchase. Purchase support is still being tested; release purchases are disabled in the current build.

RevenueCat uses a generated app-user identifier rather than a Sora Fit account or your email address. Its service processes purchase records and technical information, such as device and operating-system information, to manage access and provide purchase reporting. An identifier described as anonymous by the SDK is not a promise that all related information is non-personal.

Apple handles payment credentials. Sora Fit does not receive your payment-card number. We do not attach weight, BMI, body-fat, habit records, or mirroring eligibility responses to RevenueCat customer attributes. Changing an age group does not cancel an existing purchase or create a new charge. When eligible to use the app again (18+), use the same store account and choose Restore Purchases if valid ad-free access is missing; restoring is free. Restoring purchases restores purchase access, not tracking history.

See Apple's privacy policy and RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Advertising

Current implementation: live advertising is disabled. A development-only, explicitly enabled preview can request Google test banners. Test ads are not a promise that no technical information is transmitted: when the ad SDK is initialized or a test ad is requested, Google may process device and network information.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, device identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic or performance information when initialized for a test ad. We do not pass your tracking records or mirroring responses to the advertising SDK.

The current app does not yet implement the planned consent form or a privacy-options control. This policy does not represent those controls as available. Before enabling live advertising, we will provide the applicable privacy controls and update this policy to explain them. Publishing this policy does not itself enable advertising or obtain consent.

See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps using its services.

Reminders

Reminders are optional and scheduled on your device after notification permission is granted. They contain a general check-in message, not your measurements. You can turn reminders off in Sora Fit or manage notification permission in your phone's settings. This implementation does not register a remote push-notification token or send reminders from our own server.

Contacting us

If you email support, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include, such as your name, screenshots, and description of the issue. We use these to respond and resolve requests. Our support mailbox is provided by Gmail. Please avoid sending health records, payment credentials, passwords, or unnecessary personal details. We do not automatically attach your tracking records to support messages.

Retention, deletion, and security

Information on your device

Local tracking records and preferences remain in the app's local storage unless you delete them using a control that supports deletion or remove the app and its local data.

  • Weight and body-fat entries: the app lets you delete displayed weight entries and edit or delete body-fat entries.
  • Today's wins: choosing Remove hides a default or custom win from your list; it does not permanently delete its check-ins, celebration history, or custom name. Undo restores a removed win. Hidden defaults can also be restored from suggestions; adding the same custom name restores that custom win. Removing a win does not send a deletion request to a server.
  • Age group: changing your age group does not erase tracking information. Records remain on the device even when the selected age group cannot access them.
  • All local tracking data: there is currently no in-app erase-all control or permanent-delete control for an individual win's history. Deleting the iOS app and its local data removes these records from that installation. Offloading the app does not delete its data. Operating-system backups or device-transfer copies may retain records separately, and restoring a backup may bring them back.

We cannot remotely read or erase the tracking records stored only on your device.

Purchases and support correspondence

Deleting the app does not automatically delete Apple transactions, RevenueCat records, or support emails. Contact us about information held by us or on our behalf; we may need limited information to locate the relevant records and verify the request. We cannot remotely inspect or erase your local tracking history. Information may need to be retained to restore purchases, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or meet applicable recordkeeping requirements.

Our support-email retention policy is to review resolved conversations and their attachments manually each month for deletion once the issue has been closed for more than 12 months, except where an ongoing dispute or applicable legal obligation requires longer retention. This period applies only to support correspondence, not local tracking data or Apple and RevenueCat purchase records. This is a manual process, not automated deletion; the monthly review process is being established. Contact us with earlier deletion requests, which we consider subject to applicable rights and retention obligations.

Security

Local storage uses the app's device storage and is not protected by a separate Sora Fit password or app-managed encryption layer. Protect access to your device and backups. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Privacy requests

Depending on where you live and the laws applicable to the processing, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, or to object, restrict processing, withdraw consent, or opt out of particular advertising uses. Send requests or questions to sorafitsupport@gmail.com. You may also have the right to complain to a privacy regulator.

This website

Our public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no forms, advertising, or analytics scripts added by Sora Fit. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security, including when you open this policy. See GitHub's privacy statement. Clicking an email link opens your email service; sending an email shares the information described under Contacting us. Links to other providers take you to services governed by their own policies.

Changes

We will update this policy when our practices change and identify the effective date. Changes requiring additional notice or consent will be handled before the relevant new processing begins. Future accounts, cloud backup, or social features are not covered as existing features in this version.
